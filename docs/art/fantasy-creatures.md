# Art: fantasy kit creature brief

The first piece of the fantasy kit (design.md, kits; content.md 4): 46
creature pictures, the most a Classic adventure holds (design.md: up to
46 creature tiles). Started November 2026 for the remakes of RoePipi's
two *Coco's Adventures* disks (provenance.md), which keep their author's
own art but need original pictures in place of the stock creatures they
were built with. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

**Status:** roster drafted; no style sheet, no art yet.

## 1. Rules for this list

- **Ours, not a copy.** The roster is a set of common fantasy subjects
  chosen for this kit. It does not follow any builder set's names, order,
  groupings or statistics, and nothing here may be checked against one.
  Where a remake has to put our pictures where an old adventure's stock
  pictures were, the mapping lives outside the repo, with the corpus.
- **One picture, many creatures.** As in ACS, an adventure names and
  stats its creatures; a picture only has to read as the right kind of
  figure. Each row lists the roles it is meant to serve, so the 46 cover
  far more than 46 creatures.
- **Style.** Until the fantasy kit has its own style sheet, follow the
  pirate sheet's character, rendering and framing rules
  ([pirates-style.md](pirates-style.md) 3, 4 and 6): adult proportions,
  front-facing full body, faces always drawn, cel-painted, light from the
  top left, warm dark outline. People vary in gender, age, build and skin
  tone across the list; every person row states them.
- **Silhouette.** Within a band, each picture reads apart from the
  others in solid black (held item, headgear, body shape).
